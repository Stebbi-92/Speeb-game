BRELM: THE HERMIT OF NINE VISIONS   (version 1.1.0)
A first-person pilgrimage through the Book of Speeb.

You are Brelm, the Silent Cleric who cast aside his mantle to behold all Nine
Meowifestations of Speeb. Walk his path from the Plateau of Mirrors to the
Forgotten Cave, survive each trial, and witness each form of the White Cat.

    "They are not blessings. They are trials.
     Survive one, you are changed.
     Survive all... and Speeb stares back."

RUNNING
  Double-click Brelm.exe (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).
  If the screen stays black or the game will not start on an older graphics
  card, run "Brelm (compatibility mode).bat" instead.

CONTROLS
  W A S D / arrows   walk
  Mouse              look
  Left click         use what is in hand (fire, pray, light, cast, drink)
  1 to 6 / wheel     choose a relic from the hotbar
  R                  load shells
  E / F              interact (some acts must be held)
  Shift              hasten
  Ctrl / C           tread softly
  Space              leap
  Q / V              blessings (once earned)
  Tab / B            the Book of Emojis
  Esc / P            stillness (pause, options, restart trial)
  F11 / Alt+Enter    toggle fullscreen

OPTIONS (title screen and pause menu)
  Display mode (windowed, fullscreen, exclusive fullscreen), resolution,
  vertical sync, frame rate limit, anti-aliasing, field of view, head bob,
  mouse sensitivity, invert mouse Y, volume, Brelm's voice lines, difficulty.

THE PILGRIMAGE
  Prologue  The Plateau of Mirrors        Lay down the mantle; take the Vow of Severance.
  I         The Cliffs of Echoing Cries   Take nothing that is offered.             (Speebbang)
  II        The Ruins of the Fallen Kings Carve three warnings. Do not climb above.  (Emospeeb)
  III       The Verdant Wastes            Beg alms; plant grain with strangers.      (Speebchef)
  IV        The Heights of Sorrows        Climb the broken stair. Needs the Claw.    (Speebyeet)
  V         The Fields of Forgetting      Follow the pawprints. Needs the Censer.    (Glitchspeeb)
  VI        The Mirror Caves              Part the dark way. Needs the Lantern.      (Speebsnek)
  VII       The Hollow of False Comfort   Keep moving. Do not rest.                  (Floofspeeb)
  VIII      The Sealed Stone              Keep the vigil without moving.             (Runespeeb)
  IX        The Silent Lake               Tread softly.                              (Chillspeeb)
  Epilogue  The Forgotten Cave

  Hostile lesser Speebs burn with red eyes and a crimson glow. Lesser Speebs hunt Brelm in every trial land: rushers close in to bite,
  spitters keep their distance and cast bolts of pale light. Health returns
  slowly when unharmed; falling restarts the trial. Each new form you defeat
  is recorded in the Book of Emojis.

  Lesser forms come in gatherings: a set number at a time. Clear a gathering
  and you get a rest; take too long and the next gathering comes anyway.

  Before each Meowifestation is revealed, it comes in judgment and calls a
  set number of gatherings (three in most trials). Undo every form while
  surviving its attacks; if you tarry, the next gathering arrives early. Each
  Meowifestation survived grants a blessing (more health, a dash, slowed
  time, a ward and more). Blessings are listed in the Book of Emojis.

  Forms of the lesser: rushers bite; spitters cast bolts; gilded idols turn
  aside any shot to the face (flank them); small swarms come in fours; and in
  each land, after enough have fallen, a greater form crowned in red descends.
  Fallen forms sometimes leave shell offerings. Difficulty (Pilgrim, Hermit,
  Unblinking) is set on the title screen or in the pause menu.

  A saucer of the lesser sometimes descends. It hovers out of reach and lets
  down mites, tiny forms that swarm; bring it down to stop them.

  Brelm speaks a line over every form he undoes.

  The spider: a cat head on eight legs that skitters along the ground and
  leaps when close. Every hostile form now wears its emoji as a round mask
  ringed with red thorns, so you can always see which form it is.

SPLENDOUR AND REWARDS
  Litany        Undo forms in quick succession to build a Litany. Every
                fifth form brings a choir and extra faith for the Hands.
  Revelations   24 deeds to uncover, announced as they happen and listed
                in the Book of Emojis. A hundred kills earns a gilded shotgun
                (switch its finish in Options).
  The weighing  After each trial: time, kills, accuracy, falls, witnessed
                forms and judgment, weighed from Dust to Unblinking.
  Graphics      Options > Graphics quality: High adds ambient occlusion,
                bounced light and bloom.

THE HERMIT'S MARGINS
  In every land a wayfarer's shade waits near the road. Stop and hear them.
  A Leaf of Brelm's own journal lies somewhere in each land too; the ten
  Leaves are gathered in the Book of Emojis.

THE UNBLINKING EYE
  Without warning the world may stop and an eye open in the sky. Be still:
  do not move, leap or fire until it closes. Speeb rewards stillness.

  You may not be the only hermit on the road.

WALKING TOGETHER (up to four players)
  From the title, choose "Walk Together".
  To host: give the fellowship a name, keep port 24600 (or choose another),
    and press "Host a fellowship". Your gathering place shows the addresses
    others can join at. You choose the trial and the difficulty.
  To join: pick a fellowship from the list, or type the host's address
    (for example 203.0.113.7 or 203.0.113.7:24600) and press Join.
  Fellowships on the same network appear in the list by themselves.

  What is shared: the lesser forms (everyone fights the same ones), the
  steps of each trial (when one of you lays down the mantle, carves a
  warning, crosses a threshold or keeps the vigil, it happens for all),
  the Meowifestation's judgment, and the Unblinking Eye: if any one of you
  moves while it watches, all of you are seen.
  What is your own: your relics, Leaves, shades and the lesser forms you
  witness, your kills, your Litany and your own judgment.
  Falling: when your health runs out you fall but are not lost. A companion
  raises you by holding E over you. Only if every pilgrim falls does the
  trial begin again. Failing a step alone (touching a craving, sleeping in
  the Hollow, hurrying on the lake) sends back only you.
  Joining a trial already under way brings you to where the fellowship last
  stood firm; joining in the gathering place is smoother.

  Hosting for friends beyond your own network needs an open port. See
  HOSTING.txt.

THE ASHEN WASTE (open world, alone or with up to four)
  Choose "The Ashen Waste" on the title screen, or, when hosting a
  fellowship, choose it in the gathering place. Three worlds may be kept.
  You are cast out into a waste two kilometres across, with six
  settlements, nine dead beacons and some fifty ruins.

  By day, gather and build. By night (20:00 to 05:00) the forms come.
  Kindle the dead beacons with beacon oil and keep their flame through
  the vigil: the night will not come within sight of a burning beacon,
  and the burning beacons let you walk the beacon road between them.
  If you fall, what you carried is left where you fell, and you wake at
  your bedroll or the nearest settlement you have visited.

  I or Tab   your pack, and what you can make
  M          the map; it fills in as you walk
  J          the journal; click a task to follow it on the compass
  B          build: wheel to choose, R to turn, click to build,
             right click to take a piece down, B or Esc to stop
  E (hold)   gather wood, stone, scrap, salt, ash-bloom and bone;
             search crates; speak with the people of the settlements
  The world saves itself at every dawn, every two minutes, and when you
  leave. In a fellowship the host keeps the world; each of you keeps
  your own pack and map.

THE HOTBAR
  1  Shotgun          Eight pellets, a pump between shots.
  2  Praying Hands    A blast of tiny haloed Speebs that undoes every hostile
                      form before you. Recharges as you kill (watch the bar).
  3  Lantern of Vigil Found in the Mirror Caves. Light it to part the dark way.
  4  Claw of Ascent   Found at the foot of the Heights. Cast it at rings of
                      old iron to be drawn up to them.
  5  Censer           Found at the start of the Fields. Its smoke shows the
                      hidden pawprints.
  6  Bowl of Milk     Restores health. Carry up to three; saucers and greater
                      forms leave them, and sometimes others do.

  Peaceful lesser forms wear a golden halo; they are hidden in every land. Witness them to record their
  decrees in the Book of Emojis. Progress is saved after each trial; choose
  "Return to a Trial" on the title screen to revisit one.

THE BALANCE
  Speeb judges Balance. Each trial ends with a Judgment: Balanced, Tilted
  toward Excess (too much slaughter), Tilted toward Apathy (too little),
  Blind to the Lesser (no hidden form witnessed), or Found Wanting (three or
  more falls). Walk six trials in Balance and the ending changes.

THE TENTH TRIAL
  Record 60 lesser forms in the Book of Emojis (by witnessing the haloed
  ones and defeating the hostile ones) and "The Tenth Trial" appears on the
  title screen.
