# Combat

- **Read the bite.** Biting creatures gather themselves first: the head swells and glows. Then they lunge. If you are out of reach when it lands, it misses.
- **Dodge** with Q. A quick step in the way you are moving (or back, standing still). For a moment, bites pass by you.
- **Stamina.** Running, dodging and blade swings use the bar at the bottom of the screen. It refills after a breath. When it runs out you are winded and cannot run or dodge until a quarter has come back. It refills slower when you are tired, starving or in heavy armour.
- **The blade.** Press the blade key to take it in hand (4). Tap fire to swing, or hold fire and let go for a heavy blow that hits twice as hard and reaches further. Hold aim to guard: a blow from the front does a quarter of its harm but costs stamina. Raise the guard just as a blow lands (within a quarter of a second) for a **perfect guard**: it costs nothing, and the creature reels open for over a second. A landed blow holds time for a blink when playing alone; with others, the struck creature freezes for a blink and your view jolts.
- **What you carry.** Two long guns, a side arm and a blade, changed only at camp, in a town or at a workbench. See [[Arms and Clothing|Arms-and-Clothing]].
- **Heavy creatures** (gilded idols, haulers, lions) barely give ground when shot. They slow down under fire and keep coming. Rockets and heavy blows still push them back.
- **Poise.** Every creature can take so much before its footing breaks: 40% of its health, 60% for heavy ones. Every hit wears it down. Blades wear it 1.5 times faster, heavy blows and blunt weapons twice as fast, and a close shotgun blast a little faster. Left alone for two seconds, it comes back. When it breaks, the creature reels for a second and a half, loses the blow it was gathering, and takes 30% more harm. Big creatures are opened with heavy blows and teamwork.
- **Aim for the head.** Head shots do 60% more harm, and the hit marker turns gold.
- **Use the right harm.** Each creature is weak to some kinds of harm and hard against others: shotgun spread, bullets, blasts, blades and fire. See [[Creatures]].
- **Watch the sides.** Stalkers and crawlers swing wide and come at you from the flanks.
- **Great ones** each have a lair. They are much stronger, and some bring other creatures with them. Each extra player makes one 35% stronger.
- **Danger by land.** Creatures are as strong as the land they rise in, never stronger because your arms got better. Health by land danger: x1, x1.5, x2.2, x3; harm: x1, x1.3, x1.6, x1.9. The Far Shores are danger 5 after the turn: x3.6 health and x2.1 harm, champions twice as common, and kills teach more. Their plate thickens the same way as their health.
- **Working arms up.** At a workbench, guns and blades can be worked from Worn to Cleaned, Tempered and Master-worked: each step is +15% harm. A fifth step, Far-forged, is learned from a far great one (see [[Arms and Clothing|Arms-and-Clothing]]). Starting arms cost the least to work; middle arms cost half again; arms made with obsidian, salt crystal or lots of clockwork cost twice as much.
- **Beacons steady you.** Each lit beacon adds 10 most health, up to +60.
- **Armour.** Helms and body plate soak part of each blow and wear down. Broken plate covers little; mend it at a workbench (Arms, Clothing) for scrap. The thin bar above your health shows what is left.
- **Foes in plate.** The Unlit wear iron. Shots ring off it until it breaks. Aim for the head, or load Piercing Rounds. Hollow Rounds hit flesh harder but stop on plate.
- **Stay quiet.** Foes keep to their camps until they see or hear you. A bare gunshot carries over a hundred metres; a suppressed one about twenty. Sneak (Ctrl) and they see you from less far, and at night less far again.
- **Turn the blow.** A perfect guard works on any creature's bite or blow from the front.
- **Gunmen's rounds.** The Unlit gunmen and marksmen fire real rounds you can see in flight. Keep moving, or dodge (Q) as one comes: a dodge lets it pass at any range. Marksmen's rounds fly faster.
- **Heavy foes' moves.** Breakers, knights, wardens, the Hollowed, the Pale Deacon, the Tar-Drowned and the Colossus have three moves besides the plain blow, each with its own warning. A **sweep** shows a ring on the ground: step out of it. A **lunge** shows a lane toward you: step off it. A **grab** comes when they raise both arms and roar: back away or dodge, or you are held for a moment.
- **Packs.** Creatures come in mixed packs: one leader (a heavy, a caster or a howler), two to four lesser ones, and sometimes one that strikes from afar.
- **Champions.** In danger 3 and 4 lands, about one creature in fifteen is a champion, with a gold name. It has double health, better loot, and one trait: **Swift** (faster), **Plated** (iron plate), **Burning** (sets you alight), **Splitting** (breaks into two when slain) or **Warded** (shots to the body do half; aim for the head).
- **What they leave.** Slain creatures often leave a few rounds, usually for a gun you carry. They never leave healing.
- **Recoil.** Each gun pulls sideways in its own fixed way, the same every time. Pull against it, and fire in short strings.
- **Practice.** Each gun and the blade get better the more you hit with them, through ten ranks. A practised hand also sways less down the sights: at the top rank the sway is about half.
- **Sway.** Down the sights your aim sways a little, and the shot goes where the sights are. Heavy guns and rifles sway more, pistols less, and a foregrip steadies it. Walking or being winded makes it worse.
- **Warnings.** The far forms warn before their ways: dust boils where a burrower will come up, a flicker crackles behind you before it appears, the ground hisses before a geyser bursts, ravens gather over a raven mask before they fly at you, a choir is heard singing, and a stalker rustles in the grass. (The far forms walk only in the dev menu until their lands arrive.)
- **Hits you can hear.** A struck form rocks away from the blow, and each one sounds of what it is made of: flesh, iron, glass, bone or shell.
- **Bullet drop.** Past about 25 metres a round falls as it flies. Slow, light rounds (shotgun, pistols) fall most; rifle rounds hold their line longer. At range, aim a little high. Rockets fall too.
- **The scope.** Behind a scope the sway is bigger. Hold Sprint (Shift) to hold your breath and steady it for a few seconds.

- **Sound.** Guns are louder and the flamethrower quieter. Creature cries, thuds and steps are now real recordings.

Guns and their parts are changed under **Arms** in your pack; see [[Arms and Clothing|Arms-and-Clothing]]. Parts are made at a workbench, and fittings found in the waste rework them to better grades.
