# The Grey Sea

_This page is made from the game's own data by `tools/wiki.gd`. Edit the game, not this page._

Past the coast of the waste lies the Grey Sea, and four isles. Each isle is its own land, with its own foes, and a ruin at its heart that keeps a lost book.

## The isles

- **Hrafnsey**: Black sand, green moss and smoking ground. The springs here are hot enough to heal, or to scald. Its ruin is The Saga Stones. Draugar walk here (heavy dead men, best burned), and the Nykur, a grey horse by the water whose bite drags at your legs.
- **The Sunk Fen**: A drowned forest on a low isle. The water is black and the trees still stand in it. Its ruin is The Drowned Chapel.
- **Kalaja**: Pale stone hills, old olive trees and grey domes dug into every slope. Someone feared an enemy that never came. Its ruin is The Old Keep. The Shtriga hunts here at night (each bite heals her; fire undoes her), and the Kulshedra, a she-dragon that breathes fire. Get behind stone.
- **Cogwright**: An isle of iron. Pipes and wheels and chimneys, still turning for no one. Its ruin is The Engine Vault.

Each isle has a pier facing the waste, a hut by it, and two wrecks with something left aboard.

Each isle also keeps four **traces**: small scenes left by those who came before, not marked on any map. Look at one (**E**) to read it. Finding all four on an isle pays 25 coin.

## Swimming

Swimming costs breath, and running in the water costs much more. Out of breath in deep water, you drown. Something lives in the open sea, far from land: keep moving, or keep near the shore.

## Boats

| Boat | Seats | Strength | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raft | 2 | 160 | Logs lashed together. Paddle it with the walking keys. Slow, and the waves come over it. |
| Sloop | 4 | 420 | A small boat with a mast. Raise the sail and run before the wind; steer with the walking keys. |
| Longboat | 8 | 640 | A long boat with oars and a mast, for a whole fellowship. Slower to turn than a sloop. |

Make a boat at a workbench, then use it from your pack at the shore to launch it. The first aboard takes the helm. Boats run aground in the shallows. Hold the use key on a hurt boat to mend it with 4 wood.

## Lost books

Read a lost book and the whole fellowship learns what it teaches.

- **The Shipwright's Folio**: Drawings of hulls and masts, salt-stained. Teaches the Sloop and the Longboat.
- **The Furrier's Pattern**: How to cut and sew hides fur-side in. Teaches the Fur Coat and Fur Hood.
- **The Herbwife's Notes**: Pressed flowers and careful measures. Teaches a cheaper salve.
- **Storm Rigging**: Knots and sail plans for heavy weather. Your boats run a fifth faster.
- **The Salter's Receipts**: Brines and cures. Teaches salted meat that keeps, in plenty.
- **The Lamplighter's Primer**: Wicks and pitch. Teaches five torches from a little oil.
- **The Gunwright's Ledger**: Tolerances, springs and filing. Teaches rare fittings from fine ones.
- **The Smoke Saga**: A tale from Hrafnsey of fire in black glass. Teaches rockets from obsidian.
- **The Drowned Hymnal**: Its pages are still wet. Teaches a bog salve from tar.
- **The Keeper's Law**: The old law of the hill keeps of Kalaja. Teaches storied fittings from red iron.
- **The Engine Book**: Diagrams of Cogwright's wheels. Teaches fine fittings from scrap and salt crystal.
