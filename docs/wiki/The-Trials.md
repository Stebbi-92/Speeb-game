# The Trials

A pilgrimage of short trials, each a vision of the Hermit. Choose **The Trials** on the title screen.

- **Begin the Pilgrimage** starts from the first trial.
- **Return to a Trial** replays any trial you have reached.
- **The Tenth Trial** opens once the nine are done.
- **The Book of Emojis** (Tab) keeps every form you have met.

Relics found along the way give new powers, bound to the number keys. Arms for the trials are chosen under **The Trials**, and how you look under **Appearance**.
