# Seasons and Weather

_This page is made from the game's own data by `tools/wiki.gd`. Edit the game, not this page._

A season lasts 5 days. The nights grow longer or shorter with it.

| Season | Night falls | Dawn |
|---|---|---|
| The Thaw | 20:00 | 5:00 |
| High Ember | 21:00 | 4:30 |
| Ashfall | 19:30 | 5:30 |
| Salt Frost | 18:00 | 7:00 |

## Weather

- **Rain**: Rain is coming in off the sea.
- **Ash storm**: An ash storm is coming. Cover your face, or find shelter.
- **Salt frost**: The air turns bitter. Salt frost tonight: dress warm and keep near a fire.
- **Gale**: A gale is rising. The sea will run high.

An ash storm chokes anyone whose face is not covered. Salt frost bites at night unless you dress warm (a fur coat, or two warm things) or keep near a fire. In your camp or in a town, the weather does not reach you.

## The seventh night

Every seventh night the great raid comes, in three waves, for the camp you keep or for you. You are warned two nights ahead. Beat it back, and the forms leave what they carried.

Who comes changes as the beacons are lit:

| Beacons lit | The host | Its lead |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | The Hollow Pilgrimage: the Hollowed, Lost Vigils, Salt-Bones | a Gravewarden |
| 3 to 5 | The Unlit War Band: gunmen, blades, marksmen, firebrands | two Unlit Breakers |
| 6 or more | The Iron Host: Pale Deacons, Tar-Drowned, blades, the Hollowed | an Ashen Knight and a Hollow Colossus |

The day before a season turns, you are told what it brings.

## Trophy powers

Build a Trophy Shrine at your camp. Lay a great one's trophy on it once; from then on, kneel at any shrine to take up its power for half an hour. One power at a time.

- **Ember Heart** (Matron's Ember): The cold does not bite you, and ash does not choke you.
- **The Choir's Hush** (Choir Tongue): Forms see you at little more than half the distance.
- **White Breath** (White Shard): Running, swinging and swimming cost far less breath.
- **The Watcher's Eye** (Watcher's Eye): Your blows and shots strike a fifth harder.
- **Gilded Skin** (Idol's Gilt): Every blow you take is a fifth lighter.
- **Tar Lungs** (Tar Pearl): You swim without tiring, and the sea cannot drown you.
