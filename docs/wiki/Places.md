# Places

_This page is made from the game's own data by `tools/wiki.gd`. Edit the game, not this page._

## Settlements

Walled places where people live, trade and give work. Inside their walls, no form rises.

- **Cinderwick**: A walled camp of salt-diggers, the first to take in the cast-out.
- **The Lantern Well**: A village gathered about a deep well, where the last oil of the waste is drawn and rendered.
- **Saint Hesk's Crossing**: A market at the meeting of the old roads. Everything has a price here, and salt pays it.
- **The Quiet Kiln**: Smiths and potters working in the shell of a great kiln that has not been cold in forty years.
- **Mourner's Rest**: A camp among the graves, kept by those who still bury the dead in the old way.
- **Gallowmere**: A hard outpost on the dry bed of a lake. Its people put out the beacons, and they know it.
- **Saltreach**: The last salt-camp before nothing. The caravans used to turn back here.
- **Ember Hollow**: Coal-diggers in a crater that has stayed warm since the fall.
- **Last Light**: A watchtower at the far rim that never let its lamp go out.

## Lands

The waste is made of six lands, each with its own ground, its own weather and its own great one: Ash, Dunes, Salt, Forest, Basalt, Marsh.
