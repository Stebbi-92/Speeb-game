# Versions

Brelm is in alpha. Each version is on the [Releases page](https://github.com/Stebbi-92/Speeb-game/releases).

## 0.6.0 (arms and fighting)

- Arms screen with a live gun, parts by place, grades and traits; ten ranks of practice for each gun and the blade.
- Fixed recoil patterns, spread marks, visible reloads, piercing and hollow rounds.
- Sniper: iron sights until a Scope is fitted; a new scope with breath you can hold, and its own sensitivity setting.
- Armour that soaks harm, breaks and is mended at a workbench.
- New foes: the Unlit (raiders in Speeb masks), the Hollowed, Salt-Bones, Lost Vigil and Gravewarden. Foes keep to their camps until they see or hear you; suppressors keep you quiet.
- Great ones charge, rain fire and slam, with ground warnings.
- New sounds, a volume per kind of sound, and blood (can be turned off).

## 0.5.3

- Fixed: the hotbar was missing in the Ashen Waste. It now sits low in the middle with stamina above it.
- A quest card at the top left, and a cleaner health bar.

## 0.5.2

- New look for every screen: drawn pictures for every item, a new frame with tabs and a close button, and the world dimmed behind it.
- Making, Arms and Build are now grouped down the side, with a card and a button for each thing.
- The pack and chests use square slots with large pictures.
- The blade on key 3: tap to swing, hold for a heavy blow, aim to guard. A stamina bar for running, dodging and swings.
- Fixed: shot creatures no longer fly across the map. Heavy creatures slow down and keep coming.
- Fixed: typing a chest's name no longer opens the map or other screens.

## 0.5.1

- World settings for each world: difficulty, enemy skill, enemy numbers, resources, hunger, air support, ammunition and what you drop when you fall.
- Talking, trading, the journal and skills redone.

## 0.5 (polish and first steps)

- New title screen: Continue, the waste first, trials and arms in their own menus; worlds can be exported and deleted.
- First-steps guide for new survivors.
- New pack, making and chest screens: filters, tidy, details, make many at once, take everything, store what's already in a chest.
- Hold interact on a dropped satchel to take everything. Drops glow and can be seen from afar.
- Map zoom, dragging and shared marks, shown on the compass.
- Combat: telegraphed bites, dodge on Q, staggering, head shots, flanking.
- Falling: an unconscious timer, giving up, and smelling salts to raise a friend.
- Chests in a base are used when making and building.
- Floors sit evenly on slopes.
- Co-op: fellowship list on L, join and leave notices, a plain choice of who can find a hosted game. Fixes for items being copied when a joiner used a chest, and gun parts not showing to others.
- People turn to face you and their mouths move as they speak.
- A loading screen, and a question before leaving.
