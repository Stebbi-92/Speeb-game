# Versions

Brelm is in alpha. Each version is on the [Releases page](https://github.com/Stebbi-92/Speeb-game/releases).

## 0.13.0 (feel)

- **The map** is black until you have been there, sea and isles too. Lit beacons show the land about 500 m around them.
- **Hits:** struck forms rock away from the blow, and each hit sounds of what it strikes (flesh, iron, glass, bone, shell).
- **Warnings** before the far forms act: burrower dust, flicker crackle, geyser hiss, gathering ravens, a choir singing, a stalker rustling (ready for when the far lands arrive).
- **Carrying:** two long guns, a side arm and a blade. Change them only at camp, in a town or at a workbench.
- **Sights:** every sight seated on the rail, smaller dots on the Red Dot, Reflex and Holo, the Short Scope with a crosshair at 4x, the Scope and sniper scope at 8x, and the sniper's iron sights at 2x. The Belt Gun and Sniper Rifle iron sights line up. Gun parts sit where they belong, and muzzle flashes come from the muzzle.
- **Shotgun:** each reload pushes in a real 12 gauge shell, and the pump throws out the fired one.
- **Sound:** louder guns, a quieter flamethrower, and 14 sounds that were made in code replaced with real recordings.
- **Loading:** the world streams in around you (much smoother in towns), and loading shows a bar and changing hints.
- **Beacons:** the light over a lit beacon and the red smoke over a dead one are soft, with wisps rising up them.

## 0.12.0 (the road)

- Always a next goal: smoke over dead beacons and lairs, "What now" in the journal, beacon boons, beacon steps and vows.
- Roaming events, raid camps, named nights, a night pace per world, the ash-radio and ember runs.
- New creatures: the Ash Weaver, the Harrow and the Gaunt, the folklore forms of Hrafnsey and Kalaja, and a bestiary.
- Isle ground and story scenes; a natural coast for new worlds.
- Hit-stop, gathering chips, bullet drop, lined-up iron sights, moving gun parts and a roaring flamethrower.
- Camp pieces, a build menu, drag and drop, and roofs that keep the weather out.
- Fixes: the world loads in steps, version check on join, safer chest moves, stuck rescue.

## 0.11.0 (the armoury)

- Real models for every gun: HK 416 machine gun, a new Belt Gun (M60), minigun with spinning barrels, RPG, flamethrower, shotgun, revolver, pistol and rifle.
- Gun parts from a real accessories set: sights, scopes, suppressors, compensators, a muzzle brake, a flash hider and grips. Nine new parts to make at the bench.
- Guns throw spent cases of their own calibre. Piles of spilled rounds lie by ruins and near Cinderwick.
- Box Cats: screaming packs near Cinderwick at night.
- New models for the glaive, fire grenade (molotov), flare and Salt Mine.
- Textured ground, scanned trees and stones, new sounds and smoke.
- Fixes: mist no longer fills the screen at night; Escape closes the world settings; town pieces drawn in fewer batches.

## 0.10.0 (the fellowship)

- Up to eight in the Ashen Waste (four in the trials), with the waste growing harder for a bigger fellowship.
- The Longboat (eight seats) and beds that belong to one pilgrim.
- A new list of fellowships: cards, fill dots, filters, sort and each world's day and fires.
- Thirteen new forms of the far lands, ready in the dev menu until their lands arrive.

## 0.9.0 (the end of the road)

- The story's end: the Lake Stair, Lena's crossing, the Tenth Hall and four endings at the page (Keeper, Writer, Unlit, Pilgrim).
- Foes by the danger of the land, thirteen new foe kin, and dread in the open dark at night.
- The Kraken-Bell, the Lamp-Eater, the Drowned Fleet and deep vaults under the sealed vaults.
- New weapons: flamethrower, knife, sword, battle axe and obsidian glaive. Obsidian plate.
- Trades (smith, cook, alchemist, tinker), brewing, spirit-cats and settlers at your base.
- Living fires, a gentle start on the Ash Plains, and each land's own air.

## 0.8.0 (the Grey Sea)

- A coast, the Grey Sea and four isles to explore: Hrafnsey, the Sunk Fen, Kalaja and Cogwright.
- Swimming, a raft and a sloop, and something in the deep water.
- Four seasons and changing weather: rain, gales, ash storms and salt frost.
- Eleven lost books that teach new things to make.
- Ten new tales from the towns, most sending you across the sea.
- The great raid every seventh night, and trophy powers at a shrine.
- Every ruin has a short story. Each land has its own sound.

## 0.7.1 (fixes, clothing)

- Fixed a crash when starting a game in the waste (the new building models were missing from 0.7.0).
- A Wear tab with eight clothing slots. The lands' wards are now worn there.
- Gathered resources slowly come back. Foes grow tougher as your guns improve.

## 0.7.0 (the journey)

- Meals: up to three at once, each adding health, stamina or healing for a while. New foods: Bloom Tea, Bone Broth, Salt-crust Pie.
- Rest: sleep somewhere comfortable to wake rested, gaining more experience for a while.
- Lands grow harder from the ash to the basalt. Each has a hazard, a ward against it, and its own material.
- Building: new wall models, upgrades from timber to stone to iron, and home furniture.
- Towns have houses, roads and an inn with meals, beds and rumours.
- Eight new foes, some of which burn, slow or drain you. Great ones wait until you call them out at their stone.
- Bullets take time to fly beyond about 25 m. Gun parts cost more and need the lands' materials.
- Faster cats, and many playtest fixes.

## 0.6.1 (fixes, riding cats)

- The new foes can be hit anywhere on their bodies (before, only at the legs), and head shots find their real heads.
- Riding cats: tame a stray near a town with three meats, ride it, name it, armour it, and whistle (N) to call it.

## 0.6.0 (arms and fighting)

- Arms screen with a live gun, parts by place, grades and traits; ten ranks of practice for each gun and the blade.
- Fixed recoil patterns, spread marks, visible reloads, piercing and hollow rounds.
- Sniper: iron sights until a Scope is fitted; a new scope with breath you can hold, and its own sensitivity setting.
- Armour that soaks harm, breaks and is mended at a workbench.
- New foes: the Unlit (raiders in Speeb masks), the Hollowed, Salt-Bones, Lost Vigil and Gravewarden. Foes keep to their camps until they see or hear you; suppressors keep you quiet.
- Great ones charge, rain fire and slam, with ground warnings.
- New sounds, a volume per kind of sound, and blood (can be turned off).

## 0.5.3

- Fixed: the hotbar was missing in the Ashen Waste. It now sits low in the middle with stamina above it.
- A quest card at the top left, and a cleaner health bar.

## 0.5.2

- New look for every screen: drawn pictures for every item, a new frame with tabs and a close button, and the world dimmed behind it.
- Making, Arms and Build are now grouped down the side, with a card and a button for each thing.
- The pack and chests use square slots with large pictures.
- The blade on key 3: tap to swing, hold for a heavy blow, aim to guard. A stamina bar for running, dodging and swings.
- Fixed: shot creatures no longer fly across the map. Heavy creatures slow down and keep coming.
- Fixed: typing a chest's name no longer opens the map or other screens.

## 0.5.1

- World settings for each world: difficulty, enemy skill, enemy numbers, resources, hunger, air support, ammunition and what you drop when you fall.
- Talking, trading, the journal and skills redone.

## 0.5 (polish and first steps)

- New title screen: Continue, the waste first, trials and arms in their own menus; worlds can be exported and deleted.
- First-steps guide for new survivors.
- New pack, making and chest screens: filters, tidy, details, make many at once, take everything, store what's already in a chest.
- Hold interact on a dropped satchel to take everything. Drops glow and can be seen from afar.
- Map zoom, dragging and shared marks, shown on the compass.
- Combat: telegraphed bites, dodge on Q, staggering, head shots, flanking.
- Falling: an unconscious timer, giving up, and smelling salts to raise a friend.
- Chests in a base are used when making and building.
- Floors sit evenly on slopes.
- Co-op: fellowship list on L, join and leave notices, a plain choice of who can find a hosted game. Fixes for items being copied when a joiner used a chest, and gun parts not showing to others.
- People turn to face you and their mouths move as they speak.
- A loading screen, and a question before leaving.
