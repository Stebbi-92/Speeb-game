Brelm alpha 0.8.0: The Grey Sea

The sea and its isles
- The waste now ends at a coast. Past it lies the Grey Sea, and four isles, each its own land:
  Hrafnsey, with black sand, pines, hot springs and vents of scalding steam;
  the Sunk Fen, a drowned graveyard where lights drift over the water at night;
  Kalaja, with pale hills, olive trees, an old keep and siege engines facing the sea;
  Cogwright, an isle of iron, with mills, scaffolds and wheels that still turn.
- Each isle has a pier facing the waste, a hut to wait out a storm, two wrecks with something left aboard, and a ruin at its heart that keeps a lost book.
- You can swim, but it tires you. Run out of breath in deep water and you drown.
- Something lives in the deep water. Keep near the shore, or keep moving.

Boats
- Make a Raft at a workbench and paddle it, or learn to build a Sloop and sail it before the wind.
- Launch a boat from your pack at the shore. Up to four can ride a sloop; the first aboard takes the helm.
- Boats run aground in the shallows and can be wrecked. Hold the use key on a hurt boat to mend it with wood.

Seasons and weather
- Four seasons turn every five days: the Thaw, High Ember, Ashfall and Salt Frost. The nights are longer or shorter with them.
- Rain, gales, ash storms and salt frost come and go. Ash storms choke you unless your face is covered; salt frost bites at night unless you dress warm or keep near a fire.
- New warm clothes: the Fur Coat and Fur Hood.

Lost books
- Eleven lost books are hidden in ruins and on the isles. Reading one teaches the whole fellowship new things to make.

Town quest lines
- Ten new tales from seven towns, each opening the next, and most of them sending you across the sea.

The seventh night
- Every seventh night the great raid comes, in three waves, for your camp or for you. You are warned two nights ahead.
- Beat it back and the forms leave behind what they carried.

Trophy powers
- Build a Trophy Shrine. Lay a great one's trophy on it once, and from then on you can kneel there to take up its power for half an hour.

The land
- Every ruin has a short story to tell the first time you find it.
- Each land has its own sound: leaves in the forest, water in the marsh, birds on the green isles and insects after dark.
- Beasts now graze on the isles too.
- Towns are laid out in different ways, with houses of different sizes, a smithy, a well, stalls and a watchtower.
- The lands near where you woke are gentle, with no hardship; danger grows the further out you go. Tar slows a run instead of stopping it.
- A red line says what a land is doing to you and what to wear.
- Friends sit astride their cats properly. Press Y near a fallen friend to use smelling salts.

Fixes
- The Wear tab no longer overflows its frame.
- Fixed a script error when foes looked for a second player in co-op.

Brelm alpha 0.7.1: Fixes, clothing, and a living land

Fixes
- Fixed a crash when starting a game in the waste. The new wall and furniture models were missing from 0.7.0.
- The map is now drawn bit by bit as you play, so it never freezes and can't crash a hosted game.

Clothing (an early look at 0.8)
- A new Wear tab in the pack shows you as you stand, with eight places to wear things: head, eyes, face, neck, body, cloak, hands and feet.
- Make clothes there at a workbench, wear them, or take them off.
- The lands' wards are now worn, not carried: the Dust Veil at the neck, the Spore Mask (or a Gas Mask) on the face, Salt Goggles over the eyes, the Ember Cloak on the back and Tar Boots on the feet.
- New pieces: Wool Scarf, Travelling Cloak and Hide Gloves.

The land
- Trees, rocks and heaps you have picked clean slowly come back, a little at a time, while no one is near.
- Foes grow tougher as your guns improve, so the near lands stay dangerous after an upgrade.

Brelm alpha 0.7.0: The journey

Meals and rest
- You can have up to three meals at once. Each adds health, stamina or healing while it lasts. You can eat the same meal again once it wears thin.
- New foods made at a fire: Bloom Tea, Bone Broth and Salt-crust Pie.
- Sleep somewhere comfortable and you wake rested, gaining more experience for a while. A fire, a bed, a roof, walls, a light and furniture all add to the comfort, and an inn bed is best of all.

The lands
- Each land is harder than the last. The ash is gentlest; the dunes and forest are harder, then the salt and marsh, and the basalt is hardest. Foes there have more health and hit harder.
- Each land has its own danger: heat in the dunes, spores in the forest, glare on the salt, tar in the marsh, burning stone on the basalt. Make its ward at a workbench to be safe: Dust Veil, Spore Mask, Salt Goggles, Tar Boots or Ember Cloak.
- Each land has its own material: red iron, heartwood, salt crystal, tar and obsidian. The better gun parts need them.

Building
- New walls, doorways and windows with proper models.
- Build mode: press Interact on a wall to upgrade it from timber to stone to iron. Each is much tougher than the last.
- Home pieces: bed, table, chair, shelf (a chest), iron stove (a cooking fire), rug and standing lamp.

Towns and inns
- Towns have houses, roads and an inn. At the inn, buy a meal, rent a bed, or listen for rumours that mark a ruin on your map.

Foes
- Eight new foes: the Unlit Marksman, the Unlit Firebrand (sets you burning), the Pale Deacon (heals as it bites), the Tar-Drowned (slows you), the Hollow Colossus, the Ember Wisp, the Ashen Knight (in plate) and the Salt Rifleman.
- Great ones no longer wake as you walk up. Call one out at its stone when you are ready.

Guns
- Bullets now take time to fly. Beyond about 25 metres you must lead a moving target.
- Gun upgrades and parts cost more, and the better ones need the lands' materials.

Riding
- Your cat now walks faster than you can run and sprints much faster. Its breath lasts twice as long and comes back quicker.
- Feed a hurt cat meat to ride it again at once.
- A tamed cat stays visible where you left it, for you and for friends.
- You sit on top of the cat now, not inside it. You can't mine while riding.

Fixes
- Sprinting uses more stamina, jumping now uses some, and the stamina bar always shows when it is not full.
- The Salt-Pan Caravans and other clearing quests keep sending forms until the count is made.
- The pack screen scrolls when it is full, so the Make, Arms and Build tabs stay in reach.
- The map no longer freezes the game the first time it opens.
- Quest text no longer hides behind the hotbar. The crosshair hides while aiming down the sight.
- The first-steps tip about dodging now clears when you dodge.
- Scrolling in the settings no longer turns the hotbar, and the mouse stays visible.
- Choosing a part in the Arms screen keeps your place in the list.
- Window walls are the right way up, and you can shoot through the windows.

Brelm alpha 0.6.1: Fixes, and riding cats

Fixes
- The Salt-Bones, the Hollowed, the Lost Vigil, the Gravewarden and the Unlit can now be hit anywhere on their bodies. Before, only shots at their legs struck them.
- Head shots on these foes now aim at their real heads.

Riding cats (an early look at 0.7)
- Great stray cats with Speeb faces roam near the towns by day. Feed one meat three times and it is yours.
- Ride it with Interact. It walks a little faster than you, and Hasten runs it fast until it is out of breath.
- Hold Interact on your cat to name it and give it barding of hide, bone or iron.
- It waits where you leave it. Whistle (N) and it comes.
- Harm while riding is shared with your cat. If it is hurt too much, it throws you and must rest.
- Friends see your cat under you.

Brelm alpha 0.6.0: Arms and fighting

Guns
- A new Arms screen: your gun turns slowly as it is fitted, with every part it can take, grouped by place.
- Make barrels, stocks and receivers at a workbench. Fittings found in the waste rework a part to Fine, Rare or Storied, each with its own traits.
- Each gun kicks sideways in its own fixed way, so you can learn to hold it. Marks around the dot show how wide your shots may stray.
- Practice: every gun and the blade get better the more you use them, through ten ranks.
- Two new loads: Piercing Rounds go through armour; Hollow Rounds hit flesh harder but stop on plate.
- Reloads you can see: the magazine drops out and a fresh one clicks home. The shotgun takes shells one by one.
- The sniper rifle has iron sights until you fit a Scope. Rifles from older saves keep theirs.
- A new scope: a clearer glass and crosshair, with marks for the drop. Your aim drifts with your breath; hold Hasten (Shift) to steady it for a few seconds.
- Looking down a sight or scope now turns the view at the same pace on screen as from the hip. A new setting, "Aiming and scope sensitivity", tunes it.

Armour
- Iron Helm, Bone Mail, Scrap Plate and the new Salt-crust Plate soak harm until they break. Mend them at a workbench for scrap. A thin bar above your health shows what is left.
- Head shots go over armour.

Foes
- The Unlit: raiders in Speeb masks. Gunmen keep their distance and shoot. Blades raise a guard against your gun. Breakers carry plate and a great maul. They camp by the roads.
- The Hollowed, the Salt-Bones, the Lost Vigil and the Gravewarden walk the ruins and the night.
- Foes of the waste keep to their camps until they see or hear you. Gunfire carries far; a suppressed shot hardly at all. One that spots you calls the rest, they search where they last saw you, and a badly hurt Unlit runs, then comes back.
- Turn a blade's blow with your guard and it reels open.
- Great ones charge, rain fire and slam the ground, with warnings on the ground first. After a charge they stand spent and take more harm, and at half health they grow wroth.

Sound and blood
- New sounds throughout: real gunshots (with distant versions), blades, hits, parries, roars, footsteps that change with the ground, and the sound of towns, nights and caves.
- Volume for guns, creatures, world and menus, each on its own slider.
- Blood when you hit something. You can turn it off in Settings.

Brelm alpha 0.5.2

Screens
- A new look everywhere: every item has its own picture, screens have a cleaner frame with tabs and a close button, and the world dims behind them.
- Making: groups down the side (healing, food, ammunition, tools, materials). Each thing has a card showing what it takes, with a Make button.
- Arms and Build: the same cards and groups, with bars for harm, fire rate, reach and rounds.
- The pack and chests use square slots with large pictures.

Fighting
- Press 3 to take your blade in hand. Tap fire to swing. Hold fire and let go for a heavy blow. Hold aim to guard.
- A stamina bar for running, dodging and blade swings. Run it dry and you are winded for a moment.

Fixes
- Shot creatures no longer fly across the map. Heavy creatures like the gilded idols slow down under fire and keep coming.
- Typing a chest's name no longer opens the map or other screens.

World settings

Screens

- Work offers show their rewards plainly.
- Trading: buy one, five or all you can afford; sell one or all; sell all trinkets at once.
- Journal: a details column, and Show on the map.

Brelm alpha 0.5

Polish and first steps. Everything here is in the Ashen Waste unless it says otherwise.

Getting started
- A new title screen: Continue, the waste first, the trials and arms in their own menus.
- Worlds can be exported to a zip or deleted, and the saves folder opened.
- New survivors get a short first-steps guide. Turn it off in Settings.
- A loading screen, and a question before you leave.

Screens
- The pack has filters, a tidy button, icons and a details column. Right click eats, uses or wears.
- Making groups recipes by where they are made, shows what you have and need, and can make many at once.
- Chests in your base count when making and building.
- Chests: take everything (R), store what the chest already holds, and name them.
- Hold interact on a dropped satchel to take everything that fits.
- Drops glow: gold, amber for rare things, blue for your own belongings.
- The map zooms and drags. Right click marks a spot for everyone, also on the compass.

Fighting
- Bites are telegraphed: the head swells and glows before it lands. Step out of reach and it misses.
- Dodge with Q.
- Heavy harm, or a blade, staggers a creature.
- Head shots do more, with a gold hit marker.
- Stalkers and crawlers come at you from the sides.

Falling
- An unconscious timer. Hold jump to give up and wake at your bed.
- Smelling salts raise a fallen friend faster and stronger.

Together
- The player list is on L, and shows a fallen friend on its own. It no longer covers your task.
- Joins and leaves are shown on screen.
- A plain choice of who can find a hosted game.
- Fixed: items were copied when a joiner put things in a chest.
- Fixed: gun parts did not show to other players.

Also
- People turn to face you and their mouths move as they talk.
- Floors sit evenly on slopes.
- Controller support (all modes).
- A wiki: github.com/Stebbi-92/Speeb-game/wiki

Brelm alpha 0.4

The Ashen Waste grows. Everything here is in the open world, alone or with a fellowship.

A bigger waste
- Four times the land: 4 km across, with three new towns in the far reaches (Saltreach, Ember Hollow, Last Light).
- Roads join the towns, with signposts. Landmarks on the skyline: great bones, rock spires, old towers.
- Things happen on the road: cold camps, fallen travelers, cairns, packs of forms crossing.
- Your old worlds keep their towns, beacons and ruins.

Living off the land
- Hunt Ash Hares, Dune Goats and Ash Tuskers for meat and hide.
- Build a Cooking Station to cook. Hunger and rest matter; eat and sleep at camp.
- Farm tubers and herbs in planters.
- Campfires, cooking stations and torches burn wood: a piece a day, up to ten days.

Camps
- A lit campfire makes a base. No forms rise near it, and towns are safe at night.
- Now and then a camp is raided at night. A bell rings first.
- Chests, torches, stairs, windows, palisades, and waystones: two with the same name link.
- Skip the night when everyone lies down by a fire.

Arms
- No menu at the start: make, fit and improve guns at a workbench.
- Several attachments per gun. Iron sights on every gun. Red dots and scopes can be looked through.
- Hip fire is less accurate; aim down the sights.
- Make every kind of ammunition at a workbench.
- Armour and clothing for head, body and feet.
- Choose which kill streak to call from the air support menu.
- The Claw of Ascent grips any wall or rock. The Censer's smoke shows and slows forms. Flares reveal what moves.

Forms
- New kinds with names, strengths and weaknesses: the Bone Hauler, the Howler, the Ember Sack, the Dune Lurker.

People
- Each person in the waste speaks in their own voice. Townsfolk go about their day.
- A quest tracker shows how far and which way.

Together
- Drop and give items to each other.
- When anyone takes up a quest, everyone hears and follows it.
- Join a game in progress and arrive beside the others. Leaving keeps where you were and what you carried.
- Show what you are playing in Discord, and join friends from there.

Also
- Longer days and nights.
- Clicking in a satchel no longer fires your gun.
- Leaving a game no longer garbles the menu.
