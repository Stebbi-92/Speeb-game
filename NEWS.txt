Brelm alpha 0.12.0: The Road

Always a next goal
- Red smoke rises over dead beacons and dark smoke over lairs that wait. "What now" at the top of the journal names the next goal, what is near, and how to grow.
- Each beacon you light gives a gift: choose one of three boons. It also shows the next dark beacon and the next land's lair on the horizon.
- Beacon steps show the next small goal. Vows can be sworn when a world begins (Glass, Hunted, Tithe).
- Waking from a fall shows the nearest lair or ruin you have not found.
- Guiding embers drift toward the goal you track now and then (toggle in Settings).

Nights and the road
- Roaming events every few minutes: a caravan under attack, a fallen star, a supply cache, a Box Cat swarm. Ignore one and it may grow into a raid camp.
- Named nights: the Screaming Night, the Long Dark, the Hunter's Moon and a Quiet Night. Hold until dawn and the morning pays.
- The night eases after the fellowship falls and grows as beacons are lit. Choose the pace per world: Steady, Normal or Chaos.
- The ash-radio brings calls for help nearby and warns of the night at dusk.
- Ember runs: in company, carry an ember from a lit beacon to a dark one while the others guard you.

New creatures
- The Ash Weaver, a spider the size of a cart, in the forest and the marsh.
- The Harrow, a man with a spider's legs on his back, far out in the ash and the forest. It comes only when you look away.
- The Gaunt, thin and taller than a man, waits under the ash of the dunes and the basalt.
- The old tales of the isles: draugr and nykur on Hrafnsey, shtriga and kulshedra on Kalaja.
- A bestiary page the first time each kind falls to you.

The isles
- Hrafnsey and Kalaja have their own ground. Each isle hides four small story scenes to find.
- New worlds get a natural coast with rounded corners and deep bays.

Feel and arms
- A landed blade holds time for a blink. Gathering throws chips and the node shivers.
- Rounds fall past about 25 m: pistols and shot most, rifle rounds least. Rockets fall too.
- Iron sights line up on the pistols, revolver and RPG; the RPG's rear sight hole lines up with its front hood.
- Guns move: pump forends, slides, magazines out and in. Sway down the sights, which practice steadies.
- The flamethrower roars while held, glows at the nozzle and throws rolling smoke.
- The Auto Pistol (9mm).

Building and the pack
- Camp pieces: chest, bench, bedroll, tent, barrel, anvil and a fire pit, with new models.
- While building, the pack key opens the pieces to pick from.
- Drag things between chest and pack. Choose how much a click moves (all, half, 10 or 1).
- Rain and snow stop under a roof; a sealed base keeps the storm's haze out.
- Worn and carried clothes say what they are made from.

Fixes
- The world loads in steps, and the loading words move on as it does.
- Joining checks the game version. Foes are only sent near each player. Chest moves can no longer lose or copy things.
- A pilgrim stuck below ground is set back on top.
- Leaving after hosting no longer waits on the router. A crashed player drops out in seconds.
- Town floors meet the ground with a step to the door. Doorways on the isles can be walked through.
- Mist, marsh and storm together can no longer white out the screen.
- Saves are written in the background.
