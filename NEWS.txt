Brelm alpha 0.5

Polish and first steps. Everything here is in the Ashen Waste unless it says otherwise.

Getting started
- A new title screen: Continue, the waste first, the trials and arms in their own menus.
- Worlds can be exported to a zip or deleted, and the saves folder opened.
- New survivors get a short first-steps guide. Turn it off in Settings.
- A loading screen, and a question before you leave.

Screens
- The pack has filters, a tidy button, icons and a details column. Right click eats, uses or wears.
- Making groups recipes by where they are made, shows what you have and need, and can make many at once.
- Chests in your base count when making and building.
- Chests: take everything (R), store what the chest already holds, and name them.
- Hold interact on a dropped satchel to take everything that fits.
- Drops glow: gold, amber for rare things, blue for your own belongings.
- The map zooms and drags. Right click marks a spot for everyone, also on the compass.

Fighting
- Bites are telegraphed: the head swells and glows before it lands. Step out of reach and it misses.
- Dodge with Q.
- Heavy harm, or a blade, staggers a creature.
- Head shots do more, with a gold hit marker.
- Stalkers and crawlers come at you from the sides.

Falling
- An unconscious timer. Hold jump to give up and wake at your bed.
- Smelling salts raise a fallen friend faster and stronger.

Together
- The player list is on L, and shows a fallen friend on its own. It no longer covers your task.
- Joins and leaves are shown on screen.
- A plain choice of who can find a hosted game.
- Fixed: items were copied when a joiner put things in a chest.
- Fixed: gun parts did not show to other players.

Also
- People turn to face you and their mouths move as they talk.
- Floors sit evenly on slopes.
- Controller support (all modes).
- A wiki: github.com/Stebbi-92/Speeb-game/wiki

Brelm alpha 0.4

The Ashen Waste grows. Everything here is in the open world, alone or with a fellowship.

A bigger waste
- Four times the land: 4 km across, with three new towns in the far reaches (Saltreach, Ember Hollow, Last Light).
- Roads join the towns, with signposts. Landmarks on the skyline: great bones, rock spires, old towers.
- Things happen on the road: cold camps, fallen travelers, cairns, packs of forms crossing.
- Your old worlds keep their towns, beacons and ruins.

Living off the land
- Hunt Ash Hares, Dune Goats and Ash Tuskers for meat and hide.
- Build a Cooking Station to cook. Hunger and rest matter; eat and sleep at camp.
- Farm tubers and herbs in planters.
- Campfires, cooking stations and torches burn wood: a piece a day, up to ten days.

Camps
- A lit campfire makes a base. No forms rise near it, and towns are safe at night.
- Now and then a camp is raided at night. A bell rings first.
- Chests, torches, stairs, windows, palisades, and waystones: two with the same name link.
- Skip the night when everyone lies down by a fire.

Arms
- No menu at the start: make, fit and improve guns at a workbench.
- Several attachments per gun. Iron sights on every gun. Red dots and scopes can be looked through.
- Hip fire is less accurate; aim down the sights.
- Make every kind of ammunition at a workbench.
- Armour and clothing for head, body and feet.
- Choose which kill streak to call from the air support menu.
- The Claw of Ascent grips any wall or rock. The Censer's smoke shows and slows forms. Flares reveal what moves.

Forms
- New kinds with names, strengths and weaknesses: the Bone Hauler, the Howler, the Ember Sack, the Dune Lurker.

People
- Each person in the waste speaks in their own voice. Townsfolk go about their day.
- A quest tracker shows how far and which way.

Together
- Drop and give items to each other.
- When anyone takes up a quest, everyone hears and follows it.
- Join a game in progress and arrive beside the others. Leaving keeps where you were and what you carried.
- Show what you are playing in Discord, and join friends from there.

Also
- Longer days and nights.
- Clicking in a satchel no longer fires your gun.
- Leaving a game no longer garbles the menu.
