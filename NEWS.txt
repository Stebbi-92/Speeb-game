Brelm alpha 0.3

One game holds it all now: the nine trials alone, the trials with a fellowship, and the Ashen Waste alone or together.

The Ashen Waste
- An open world of seven lands, with settlements, ruins, beacons, crafting and building.
- Experience, levels to 30 and four skill trees: Survival, Arms, Craft and Faith (K).
- The world saves itself every few minutes, on events, and when the game closes.

Arms
- Seven guns, three blades, two grenades and three gadgets, chosen before each trial.
- Sights and a suppressor that are earned with rank, up to rank 40.
- Kill streaks: an airstrike, a gun run and a gunship (H).
- The host of a fellowship may grant unlimited ammunition.

Appearance
- Choose how the fellowship sees you, from the title screen.

The lesser forms
- They now wear the heads of cats. The old emoji masks remain in Options.
- In later trials and on waste nights, something else walks that can hardly be seen. Do not look away from it.

The game now updates itself from the title screen.
